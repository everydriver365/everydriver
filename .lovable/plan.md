## Why speed limits are wrong\n\nInvestigated the instructor mobile tracking page (`/instructor/tracking` → `InstructorLiveSession.tsx` → `LiveTrackingMap` / `GlanceableTrackingMode` / `SpeedLimitRoundel`). The UI is correct — it just renders whatever km/h value comes from the resolver. The bad numbers come from `supabase/functions/_shared/speedLimitLookup.ts` and the local cache layer. There are five concrete problems:\n\n1. **Picks the first OSM way returned, not the nearest.** Overpass `way(around:100,…)` returns ways in arbitrary order. On a residential street next to an A-road we often grab the wrong one.\n2. **100 m search radius is too wide.** A 30 mph side road and a 40 mph main road frequently both sit inside the circle.\n3. **UK defaults are too generous for urban roads.** `primary/secondary/tertiary/unclassified` all default to 97 km/h (60 mph). In built-up areas these are 30 mph. OSM tags `lit=yes` and `maxspeed:type=GB:urban` are ignored, so a lit residential A-road shows 60 mph.\n4. **`trunk` defaults to 113 km/h (70 mph)**, but most single-carriageway trunk roads in the UK are 60 mph.\n5. **Cache cell is ~111 m and TTL is 30 days.** Once a wrong value lands in `speed_limit_cache` (or the IndexedDB mirror in `src/lib/speedLimitCach
